    Our Humble Beginnings

    I decided it was time to contribute my passion for repair and care of retro game consoles and technology back to the community, my dream is to provide quailty bundles and repair/modding services to anyone who needs them, thus we transitioned from this being our hobby to a passionate startup!

  • 01-26-2026

    A Retro Care Center is Born

    We made our first bulk order of dead consoles for repair, our choice being the Nintendo Wii, with the plan to give them all some very much needed TLC and to find each system their next caring user!

  • 02-23-2026

    Transition to Full Service

    As we finished repairing our initial Nintendo stock with success, we decided to start salvaging as many Xbox360 units among other seventh generation consoles for repair and parts as we could get, many of which we plan to mod after repair!

    Our Website is live and eShop is online waiting for someone like you to order, we have an ever growing selection of "launch like" quailty bundles, modded systems, and more available! Eeverything to a standard of care and skill we would expect for our own personal collections.
